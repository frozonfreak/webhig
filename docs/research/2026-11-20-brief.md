## Review rationale and compatibility

- **User harm:** A cross-origin sign-in or payment widget can open a dialog and leave a keyboard or screen-reader user outside it. A control on a hinge is hard to touch. An agent that reports a success response as completion can leave the wrong record changed, or no record changed. A confidence radius drawn as a hard boundary can send someone to the wrong place. Adding a rule for a proposal or an Editor’s Draft would require work that no standard asks for.
- **Capability condition:** The focus guidance applies to embedded frames when an embedder uses the policy, in browsers that implement it. The posture guidance applies when a product adapts to a folded device. The agent guidance applies to systems that execute state-changing tool calls. The geolocation guidance applies to location features.
- **Evidence:** Primary sources are linked above. The two papers are preprints with limited configurations and are not general measurements. Fixture execution is pending. No cross-browser claim is made for the focus policy or the Device Posture API.
- **Alternatives:** An embedder may leave the focus policy allowed for frames that must manage focus, or deliver focus by delegation after a user action. A product may omit a posture-specific layout and ship only the continuous layout. A team may verify a low-consequence, reversible change by sampling.
- **Test cases:** Open a dialog and a popover in an iframe with the policy denied from a click and from a timer, and confirm keyboard reachability, dismissal, and focus return. Switch between continuous and folded postures with WebDriver, and check placement, focus order, announcements, and reflow. Change a field name, a unit, or a priority direction behind an unchanged success response, and confirm the agent does not report completion. Give an ambiguous reference and confirm a question. Request a position with permission denied, with a timeout, and from cache, and confirm the manual fallback.
- **Exceptions:** No new rule ID, severity, applicability, WCAG mapping, or Core Web Vitals threshold. No new scanned-site rule for agent behavior, image-text contrast, focus policy, or posture. No `listview`, `text-autospace-size`, View Transitions `shadowRoots`, or Web Install requirement.
- **Compatibility:** Backward-compatible informative guidance. **HIG-A11Y-008** keeps its meaning. **HIG-A11Y-005** and **HIG-A11Y-008** gain consumption notes dated 2026-11-20. **HIG-CQ-001** and **HIG-CQ-002** are unchanged. Quick remains 98 imperatives. The registry remains 76 stable IDs.

The separate audit site is not implemented or deployed by this repository change. Its checks should follow §8.3. Fixture results must be recorded separately.
