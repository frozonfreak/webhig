## Review rationale and compatibility

- **User harm:** Treating an experimental SPA series as the stable Core Web Vitals result can hide a real field miss, or fail a route that still meets the stable gate. Unguarded scroll-linked motion keeps moving for people who asked for reduced motion. Re-rendered live regions can repeat or drop streamed text. An engine fix for name calculation does not repair other browsers.
- **Capability condition:** The experimental metrics matter for reports that display CrUX, especially SPA routes. Scroll-driven guidance applies wherever `animation-timeline`, `scroll()`, `view()`, or a script scroll/view timeline is used. Safari fixtures apply to streamed status and to names or descriptions taken from complex containers.
- **Evidence:** Primary sources are linked above. Browser and assistive-technology execution of the new fixtures is pending. No measured accessibility pass is claimed. Chrome's May 2027 graduation aim is their schedule, subject to feedback, not a HIG commitment.
- **Alternatives:** Sites can keep using stable CrUX and their own RUM. Scroll-driven effects can be omitted, wrapped in `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, or neutralized with `animation-timeline: auto` or `animation: none`. Streamed status can append or re-render; both must be tested. Native date, time, and color inputs stay preferable to custom widgets.
- **Test cases:** Read a CrUX report and confirm the stable and experimental series, the dataset month, and the experimental label are separate. Load the scroll fixture with reduced motion off and on, then with keyboard, 200% zoom, and the programmatic button. In Safari 27.2 and one other browser/AT pair, step the streamed fixture through append and re-render and inspect the complex name/description fixture.
- **Exceptions:** No new stable-metric threshold. No combined pass/fail. No Baseline claim for scroll-driven animations. No author failure solely because one engine omitted referenced content or repeated a live region. No Grid Lanes audit rule. No new AI or WCAG requirement.
- **Compatibility:** Backward-compatible clarification of **HIG-PERF-001**, **HIG-PERF-002**, and **HIG-A11Y-001**. No new IDs, severity changes, or Layer 0 changes. Quick remains 98 imperatives. The registry remains 76 stable IDs. The partial static check can newly report scroll-driven CSS that only shortens duration.

The separate audit site is not implemented or deployed by this repository change. Its performance view should follow §6.3. Fixture results must be recorded separately.
